## The Human Condition

Suppose you have never tasted water in life.  ..

Have you ever wondered why most of humanity is unable to sit, and focus on simple "boring" things for long periods of time, even when the body is comfortable ? Why do we classify "watching paint dry" as the ultimate boring activity ? Why can't we just pay attention and watch nails grow, or a flower go from bud to blossoming ? What is it inside of us that constantly needs new, shiny things to divert our attention to ?
